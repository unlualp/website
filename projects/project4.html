About Me

SciCope: AI-Driven Microscope Controller

Running an experiment in the lab used to mean juggling vendor software for the camera, a separate program for the stage and another one for the analysis. SciCope is a desktop app (Mac and Windows) I built to put the whole bench into one window: cameras, a motorised stage, the lights and a focus lens, all driven from your own computer.

Experiments can run themselves: plate scans, timelapses, autofocus and following a moving animal on the stage. An assistant can operate the microscope from plain-language requests, and any phone can be used as a camera through the companion app.

The second half of the app is an analysis workspace for finding, tracking and measuring the animals in the recordings, and it points out the moments where the tracker got confused so they can be fixed with a click.

More at scicope.com.
