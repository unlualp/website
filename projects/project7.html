Smart Heater Controller

I wanted to control my heater remotely and on a schedule, without replacing it. So I attached a servo to the heater's valve and controlled it with an ESP32 and a temperature sensor.

The controller follows a weekly schedule and works as a simple thermostat, keeping the room above a minimum temperature. I can change the mode, the schedule and the temperature from a small web page on my phone, from anywhere, and the ESP32 closes the valve on its own if it loses the connection.
