About Me

Omniverto: A Self-Hosted File Converter

Most online file converters upload your files to someone else's server. Omniverto is a free converter that you can run yourself, with a simple web interface.

It converts images, documents, audio and video between dozens of formats, and also has small editors in the browser for images, videos and LaTeX. It is written in Python and runs in Docker.
